EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2024,
between:

EMPLOYER: Nexus Technologies Private Limited, having its registered office at
14th Floor, Pinnacle Tower, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051
("the Company")

and

EMPLOYEE: Alex Morgan, residing at the address on record with the Company
("the Employee")

================================================================================

1. EMPLOYMENT AND COMMENCEMENT

1.1 The Company hereby appoints the Employee as Software Engineer - Backend
Systems, and the Employee hereby accepts such appointment on the terms and
conditions set out in this Agreement.

1.2 The employment shall commence on 1 March 2024 (the "Commencement Date").

1.3 The Employee's employment is subject to satisfactory completion of
reference checks, verification of academic qualifications, and execution of
this Agreement by both parties.

1.4 The Employee confirms that they are not subject to any contractual
obligations with a prior employer that would prevent or restrict the
performance of duties under this Agreement.

================================================================================

2. PROBATION PERIOD

2.1 The Employee's employment shall be subject to a probation period of six
(6) months commencing from the Commencement Date ("Probation Period").

2.2 During the Probation Period, either party may terminate this Agreement by
providing fourteen (14) days' written notice.

2.3 The Company reserves the right to extend the Probation Period by a
further three (3) months, subject to written notice to the Employee prior to
the expiry of the initial Probation Period.

2.4 Upon satisfactory completion of the Probation Period, the Company shall
issue a written confirmation of regularisation within 7 business days.

================================================================================

3. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Employee shall carry out the duties assigned by the Company diligently
and to the best of their ability, follow all lawful instructions and policies,
devote their full working time to the role, and act in the best interests of
the Company at all times.

3.2 The Company may from time to time reasonably change the Employee's duties,
responsibilities, or reporting line.

3.3 The Employee may not undertake any outside employment, consultancy, or
directorship without prior written consent of the Company.

================================================================================

4. REMUNERATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 SALARY: The Employee shall receive a gross annual Cost to Company (CTC) of
INR 18,00,000 (Rupees Eighteen Lakhs), payable in equal monthly instalments of
INR 1,50,000, subject to applicable tax deductions.

4.2 PERFORMANCE BONUS: The Employee shall be eligible for an annual
performance bonus of up to 15% of the basic annual salary, subject to the
Employee meeting or exceeding performance targets as determined by the Company.
The bonus is entirely discretionary and is not guaranteed.

4.3 BENEFITS: The Employee shall be entitled to Provident Fund contributions
as per applicable law, group medical insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 per
annum for the Employee and immediate family, and annual mobile reimbursement
of INR 12,000.

4.4 SALARY REVIEW: The Company shall conduct an annual salary review. Salary
increments are at the sole discretion of the Company and are not guaranteed.

================================================================================

5. WORKING HOURS

5.1 Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a
one-hour unpaid lunch break.

5.2 The Employee may be required to work beyond standard hours as reasonably
necessary to fulfil their responsibilities, without additional compensation, as
the position is classified as a professional role.

5.3 The Company operates a hybrid working policy. The Employee is expected to
be present at the Company's Mumbai office for a minimum of three (3) days per
week unless otherwise approved in writing.

================================================================================

6. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS

6.1 ANNUAL LEAVE: The Employee shall be entitled to 18 days of paid annual
leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.

6.2 SICK LEAVE: The Employee shall be entitled to 8 days of paid sick leave
per calendar year.

6.3 CASUAL LEAVE: The Employee shall be entitled to 6 days of casual leave
per calendar year.

6.4 Unused annual leave in excess of 6 days may be encashed at the end of the
calendar year at the Employee's basic daily rate.

================================================================================

7. TRAINING COST RECOVERY

7.1 The Company may, from time to time, invest in the Employee's professional
development through external training programmes, certifications, or courses.

7.2 If the Company bears training costs exceeding INR 50,000 in any rolling
twelve-month period, the Employee agrees to the following recovery schedule:
(a) If the Employee resigns within twelve (12) months of completing such
training: 100% of the training cost to be recovered.
(b) If the Employee resigns between twelve (12) and twenty-four (24) months
of completion: 50% of the training cost to be recovered.
(c) After twenty-four (24) months: No recovery obligation.

7.3 The aggregate training cost recovery obligation shall not exceed INR
1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh) in any instance.

7.4 Recovery shall be effected by deduction from final settlement amounts or
by direct payment by the Employee within 30 days of the termination date.

================================================================================

8. NOTICE PERIOD AND TERMINATION

8.1 NOTICE PERIOD: Following the completion of the Probation Period, either
party wishing to terminate this Agreement shall provide the other with thirty
(30) days' written notice.

8.2 The Company may, at its discretion, elect to make payment in lieu of
notice (PILON), equivalent to the Employee's basic salary for the notice
period.

8.3 During the notice period, the Employee shall continue to discharge their
duties diligently, hand over all pending work and responsibilities to a
designated successor, and return all Company property including equipment,
documents, and access credentials.

8.4 TERMINATION FOR CAUSE: The Company may terminate this Agreement
immediately without notice in the event of gross misconduct, serious breach of
any term of this Agreement, conviction of a criminal offence, fraudulent or
dishonest conduct, or wilful damage to Company property.

================================================================================

9. NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 During employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following
termination, the Employee shall not directly or indirectly solicit, induce, or
attempt to solicit any employee, consultant, or contractor of the Company to
leave their engagement.

9.2 The Employee shall not solicit business from any client or customer of the
Company with whom the Employee had material contact during the twelve months
prior to termination.

9.3 This clause does not prevent the Employee from placing general
advertisements for employment not targeted at specific Company personnel.

================================================================================

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 The Employee acknowledges that in the course of employment they will have
access to Confidential Information belonging to the Company, including
technical data, software code, system architecture, client lists, pricing,
commercial strategies, financial information, and business plans.

10.2 The Employee shall keep all Confidential Information strictly
confidential, not disclose it to any third party without written consent, and
use it solely for the purposes of performing their duties.

10.3 POST-EMPLOYMENT: These confidentiality obligations shall survive
termination and shall remain in force for a period of two (2) years following
the date of termination.

10.4 The obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes publicly
available through no fault of the Employee, or is required to be disclosed by
law or court order.

================================================================================

11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 All inventions, discoveries, developments, software, designs, processes,
and works created by the Employee in the course of their employment, using
Company resources, equipment, or information, or related to the Company's
business or anticipated business shall be the sole and exclusive property of
the Company.

11.2 The Employee hereby assigns to the Company all such Intellectual Property
Rights, both present and future, and agrees to execute any documents necessary
to give effect to such assignment.

11.3 The Employee waives all moral rights in such works to the maximum extent
permitted by law.

11.4 This clause does not apply to inventions created entirely on the
Employee's own time, without Company resources, and unrelated to the
Company's business.

================================================================================

12. DATA PROTECTION

12.1 The Employee consents to the Company processing their personal data for
employment purposes including payroll, HR administration, performance
management, and statutory reporting.

12.2 The Employee shall comply with the Company's Data Protection Policy and
all applicable data protection laws.

12.3 The Employee shall not process personal data of clients or colleagues
outside the scope of their duties or retain such data after termination.

================================================================================

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 In the event of any dispute arising from or in connection with this
Agreement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through
good-faith negotiation.

13.2 If not resolved within 30 days, the parties shall submit the dispute to
mediation under the rules of the Mumbai Centre for International Arbitration.

13.3 If mediation fails, the dispute shall be referred to binding arbitration
with a single arbitrator. The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai, India.

13.4 Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking urgent
injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.

================================================================================

14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of India.

14.2 Subject to the dispute resolution provisions above, the parties submit to
the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

================================================================================

15. GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 ENTIRE AGREEMENT: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
the parties relating to the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements.

15.2 VARIATION: No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed
by both parties.

15.3 SEVERABILITY: If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining
provisions continue in full force.

15.4 NOTICES: Notices shall be in writing and delivered personally, by email
with acknowledgement, or by registered post.
